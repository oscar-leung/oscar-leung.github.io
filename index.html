About

I'm a QA automation engineer and SDET with a B.S. in Computer Science from San José State University. I've tested spacecraft software at Maxar, a statewide Salesforce rollout for the State of Illinois, AT&T carrier apps at Location Labs by Avast, and frontier AI model output at Handshake AI. I write Python, Java and TypeScript, build test frameworks from scratch, and wire them into CI.

I also freelance as a QA tester on Upwork, and in 2026 I started building my own tools. Job Radar is an AI agent that finds and scores job postings, and I tested it the way I'd test anything else: hand-labeled ground truth, precision and recall, and a judge model that checks every generated claim. Writing Selenium frameworks by hand long before AI pair-programming is why I verify AI-generated code instead of trusting it.

Outside work I mentor international students with ISMP at UC Davis, play ultimate frisbee, and bring a camera to everything.
